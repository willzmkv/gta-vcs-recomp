# Mods Switched Live Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Switching a mod on or off in F2 > Mods takes effect at once for everything it holds - textures, Vic's model
(done, c424479), and the cars, people and weapons in the scene - and the menu says plainly when something (text,
the main script) only applies after a restart.

**Architecture:** `src/game/vcs_stream.cpp` already marks every archive entry whose served bytes changed ("dirty") and
removes a dirty model where the game dresses the player, by a tail call of the game's own `CStreaming::RemoveModel`
(`FUN_003C8618`) from the `RequestModel` hook (`FUN_003CB900`) that comes back to the hook. This plan extends that to
models in use by other entities: the entities using a dirty model are taken out of the world the way the population
takes its own out (random ones only), after which the model is unused, is removed at a safe point, and comes back from
the new bytes when the game spawns it again. Mission entities are never touched: their models change at the next
mission or load. The menu part is text in `src/mods/modloader.cpp`.

**Tech Stack:** C++ (src/game, src/mods), the rig for checks.

## Task 2: Models in use reloaded (mandatory: item 10)

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/game/vcs_stream.cpp` (a per-frame step `reloadDirty()`; the hook already there)
- Modify: `src/game/vcs_distance.cpp` or a new `src/game/vcs_reload.cpp` (taking entities out: the same flags the
  crowd trim uses - peds `+0x1CC` bit 31 fade / `+0x4C` bit 0 remove; cars: the population's removal path, to find)
- Modify: `docs/MODS.md`, `docs/game-internals.md` (the removal paths and the model info's reference count)

- [ ] **Step 1: Find the model info's reference count.** In a hidden run, spawn 3 peds of one model (CREATE_CHAR) and
  read its model info (`*(0x486518)[id]`, 0x60 bytes) before / after each spawn and delete: the u16 / u32 that
  counts. Record it in game-internals.md 12g.
- [ ] **Step 2: Find the cars' removal path.** Decompile `CCarCtrl::RemoveDistantCars` and the remove-a-car call
  (`tools/eedecomp.py` on the functions the population calls past the car distance; start from 0x433378, the reader
  of the generation distance in vcs_distance.cpp's notes); the flag or call that removes one random car safely.
- [ ] **Step 3: `reloadDirty()`** once a game frame (the `CPopulation::Update` reader already hooked in
  vcs_distance.cpp gives the frame): for each dirty loaded model id (not the player's: that is the dressing path):
  - random peds of it: fade them (the crowd trim's flag), out of view first; random cars of it: the step-2 removal;
    mission entities (ped `+0x8F4 != 1`, cars with a mission flag) left alone;
  - a weapon model held by the player: after the next weapon change, or re-give it (`GIVE_WEAPON_TO_CHAR` with the
    same ammo) when the player is on foot and not shooting;
  - once the reference count is 0: RemoveModel by the hook trick (a tail call from a hooked entry point that the game
    calls every frame with no live arguments; record the call site used in game-internals.md).
- [ ] **Step 4: Check** (`tools/mods_check.py --live`): a car mod (`build/analysis` copies of the player's VC car mods)
  switched on with that car parked beside Vic: the car goes and comes back modded within 30 s or when re-spawned;
  switched off: back to VCS's. A ped skin the same. A weapon in hand the same. No crash in 10 switches.
- [ ] **Step 5: Reviewer, commit, docs** (MODS.md: what changes at once, what at the next spawn, what never live).
