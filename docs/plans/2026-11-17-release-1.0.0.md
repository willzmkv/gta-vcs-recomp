## Task P1: Police chase while driving (verify 2b5401e)

The player: with Population distance up, police cars never chased a moving car - they came once it stood still. Cause
assumed: their cars come out of the traffic's spawner, at the population distance (Population distance 100 = 3 x the
game's: 300-480 m). Change 2b5401e: while the player is wanted, the spawner keeps the game's own distance
(`src/game/vcs_distance.cpp` `chased()`; `VCS_CHASE_SPAWN=0` turns it off).

Not reproduced by `tools/chase_check.py` (the rig moves the car along a route; police kept up before the change too).

- [ ] **Step 1:** the player drives with 2-3 stars on the build with 2b5401e, with their usual settings (Population
  distance 100). Does a police car follow while driving? Note where (F3 panel position) and the stars.
- [ ] **Step 2 (if still easy):** the same drive with `VCS_CHASE_SPAWN=0` and with the population sliders at 0 (the
  game's own): if the police chase at 0 but not at 100, our population code is the cause - log the spawner's police
  generations (`CCarCtrl` police branch: find with `tools/eedecomp.py` around `0x00430290`, the spawner the
  population code hooks) and their distance to the player.
- [ ] **Step 3:** a check that drives like a player: `tools/chase_check.py --drive` with the rig's autodrive (AI driving
  the player's car, `rig.py` "autodrive") instead of `cruise`.

**Done when:** the player confirms the police chase while driving at their settings.
