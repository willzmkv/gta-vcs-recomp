**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/willzmkv/gta-vcs-recomp/compare/v1.0.0...v1.0.05

Fixes

- Windows: fixed the first-time compiler (`llvm-mingw`), CMake and Ninja download failing with `SSL: CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED` on PCs where the Windows certificate store has not cached GitHub's root certificate yet or where an antivirus / VPN intercepts HTTPS connections (#31). Downloads now fall back to Windows' built-in `curl` (Schannel) and verify the file's SHA-256 checksum directly.

Updating: on Windows, download the new `VCS.rec-Launcher-1.0.05.exe` standalone launcher or extract `VCS-Recomp-1.0.05-windows.zip` into your game folder (or open the launcher and go to **Settings → Updates → Update now**). Your saves aren't touched. On Linux, update from Settings in the same way or download the new AppImage.
