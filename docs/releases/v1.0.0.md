New: First release of the HD World fork (1.0.0)

- Built on top of upstream v0.3.0 with an expanded **Detailed city distance** (HD world), extended draw distance, fog control, traffic and pedestrian density/distance sliders, distant traffic and crowds, and an in-game **Mod Loader** (`modloader/` folders, managed live in **F2 → Mods**).
- Ships with **HD Hands** (all 14 of Vic's outfits), **HD Hud**, and the **PT-BR (VCS.rec)** Brazilian Portuguese translation bundled out of the box (no separate mod download needed — toggle any of them live in **F2 → Mods**).
- A full recompile of your disc is required on the first build (this release includes upstream recompiler fixes).

Menus, subtitles and button prompts

- The in-game settings menu (**F2**) and trainer (**F5**) have been restyled to match the launcher's Miami Vice look (#80).
- New crisp **Oswald SemiBold** subtitles and help boxes, with options for font, outline, size, shadow, background and colour in **F2 → Enhancements → Subtitles** (and in the launcher's settings), plus smooth sub-pixel letter spacing (#42, #43, #44).
- Native in-game fonts now support Portuguese tilde letters (`Ã`, `ã`, `Õ`, `õ`) so mission titles and banners render cleanly on Portuguese translations.
- Button prompts now use **Kenney's Input Prompts** for PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch and keyboard, follow your chosen control scheme (e.g. Modern controls show R2 to accelerate), and work properly on translated discs.

Controls and DualSense

- **DualSense** adaptive trigger resistance now follows **Drive / Shoot with the triggers** immediately without needing a restart.
- Enabling trigger driving or trigger shooting on a gamepad no longer blocks your keyboard bindings or mouse wheel.
- The mouse-wheel weapon switch toggle is now automatically locked with an explanation if you bind **Wheel up** or **Wheel down** to another control.

World, cutscenes and population fixes

- **HD world overlaps and missing parts:** fixed distant neighbour buildings drawing over detailed ones, duplicate sectors under two IDs (#37), missing translucent glass on multi-layer detailed buildings (#36), and missing neighbour detail before it finishes loading (#32).
- **Mission building switches:** all mission-driven building swaps (such as Phil's warehouse) now update cleanly with Detailed city distance enabled (#61).
- **Cutscenes:** fixed the dark sky during cutscene fade-ins (#38), added **PS2 cutscene framing** in **Display → Screen** (fixing Vic missing in the first frame of the airport cutscene, #40), and kept the detailed city visible in outdoor cutscenes (#76).
- **Population & chases:** improved vehicle density while standing still (#35), kept police spawning at the game's distance during chases while wanted (#41), and prevented wrecks or seated drivers from appearing as distant proxy templates (#34).
- **Texture packs:** each replacement texture is now decoded once, with a VRAM-sized cache.

Launcher

- Available on Windows as both a zip archive and a **standalone launcher executable** with the bundled mods included.
- First-time installs automatically pick a graphics preset suited to your PC's hardware.
- Added launcher settings rows for DualSense, deadzone, button prompt style and subtitle options.

Updating: on Windows, open the launcher and go to **Settings → Updates → Update now**, then restart the launcher and update the build (the first build recompiles the game once). Your saves aren't touched. You can also download the standalone launcher or extract the zip into a new folder. On Linux, update from Settings in the same way or download the new AppImage.
