New: the pause menu works with the keyboard and mouse

- The mouse cursor can now be used to navigate the in-game menu.
- Escape now opens the pause menu.
- In the pause menu, Enter selects and Escape goes back. Escape on the row of tabs closes the menu.
- The gamepad still works in the menu just as before.

Car reflections

- The stripes and lines that crawled over car paint as the camera moved are gone (#75).
- Above 1x **Internal resolution**, the reflections on cars are smoothed, so panels no longer flicker as you drive past. The PS2 has this flicker too, but at a higher resolution the rest of the picture is sharp, so the cars stood out. At 1x the reflections stay as on the PS2. There's no setting for this: the internal resolution picks it.

Widescreen

- The pause menu's own Display → **Widescreen** option is gone. It was the PS2's mode for TVs that stretch the picture, so with the game's settings (F2) → Display: **Aspect ratio** set to 16:9, it squeezed and stretched everything a second time (#52). Use **Aspect ratio** instead. A save that had it on loads with it off.
- New in the game's settings (F2) → Display: **Cutscene black bars** (on by default, as on the PS2). On a screen wider than 4:3, turn it off to see the whole picture in cutscenes (#52). At 4:3 the bars are part of the original picture, so they always stay on there. Your choice still applies when you switch back to a wider aspect ratio.
- At 16:9, the black mask around the sniper scope, the camera and the binoculars now reaches the screen edges. Before, the world showed in strips at the sides (#53).

Fixes

- Above 1x **Internal resolution**, the first letter of the pause menu's tabs lost its left edge ("stats" showed a square S).

Updating: on Windows, go to Settings → Updates → Update now in the launcher, close the launcher and start it again, then update the build. The build only compiles part of the game again and links it, so it's much quicker than a first build. Your saves aren't touched. You can also extract the zip into a new folder. On Linux, update from Settings in the same way, or download the new AppImage.
