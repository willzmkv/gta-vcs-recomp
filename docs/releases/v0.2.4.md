**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/gothamor/gta-vcs-recomp/compare/v0.2.3...v0.2.4

New: an Enhancements page in the game's settings (F2)

- A new page for options that change how the game looks or plays. Each one starts at the original game's behaviour, so nothing changes until you change it.

Button prompts for keyboard, mouse and Xbox

- New in the game's settings (F2) → Enhancements: **Button prompts** (**Original (PS2)** by default). The game's help text and the pause menu's buttons can now show your keyboard keys and mouse buttons, Xbox buttons or PlayStation buttons instead of the PS2's (#61).
- **Keyboard & mouse** shows the keys you actually bound, so a key you changed shows up in the help text. In a vehicle it shows your driving keys, and in the pause menu Enter and Escape.
- **Automatic** follows what you played with last: pick up the gamepad and the prompts switch to its buttons, touch the keyboard and they switch back. A help box that's already on screen switches too.
- **Original (PS2)** shows the game's own text, as before.

Busier streets and a longer draw distance

- New in the game's settings (F2) → Enhancements → World: **Traffic density**, **Pedestrian density** and **Draw distance**, each from 100% (the original game, the default) up to 300%.
- Missions that clear the streets still do, and missions always find room for the cars and people they need.
- Pedestrians scale up well. Traffic only grows a little, because the places where cars can appear limit it more than the setting does.
- With any of these above 100%, cars in front of you are no longer removed while you can see them. The game removes cars that are far away, and with a longer draw distance you could see them vanish.
- Busier streets cost some CPU time, a longer draw distance costs GPU time.

Combat

- New in the game's settings (F2) → Enhancements → Combat: **Drive-by fire rate**. People shooting at you from cars (the Scorpion in later chases) fired one shot every frame. That's a bug in the original game: 30 shots a second on the PS2, against the gun's 11, and at 60 or 144 fps it got two to five times worse, so a car was wrecked in a couple of bursts (#94).
- **As on the PS2** (the default) keeps them at the PS2's 30 shots a second at any frame rate. **The gun's own rate** makes them fire as fast as the gun does elsewhere (11 a second for the Scorpion), which makes chases much easier to survive.

Updating: on Windows, go to Settings → Updates → Update now in the launcher, close the launcher and start it again, then update the build. The build only compiles part of the game again and links it, so it's much quicker than a first build. Your saves aren't touched. You can also extract the zip into a new folder. On Linux, update from Settings in the same way, or download the new AppImage.
